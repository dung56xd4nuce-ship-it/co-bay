🗺️ 100-stage campaign

Stages 1–20: 4 factions. Stages 21–40: 8 factions, plus 2 side flags 🚩 and whole-faction surrender. Stages 41+ (12 factions) in development. Every stage: at minute 10 a 🌩️ fog storm closes in on the Drum.

👑 BATTLE

Warlords: dozens of nations fight each other on a huge map. A nation falls only when it loses its last camp — its land and armies then surrender to the conqueror. Be the last one standing!

📶 P2P — 2-PLAYER BATTLE

Connect two devices directly over Wi-Fi (same network or Internet) by exchanging codes (browsers do not allow play over Bluetooth). The host commands the BLUE army on the left, the guest commands the right side.

📜 THE ART OF WAR — HOW TO PLAY

Commanding: tap a BLUE army (or one of your camps), then tap anywhere: tap an outpost to attack or garrison it, tap empty ground to march there and ⛺ camp in the field, tap an enemy army to 🐎 pursue it. A menu appears for every order. Gray neutral posts with fewer troops defect to you (their troops join).

👆 Controls

Drag to scroll (you can drag half a screen past the map edge to reach corners). Pinch or ➕➖ to zoom. Double-tap an army to grab its nearby group (👥 xN badge). 🔲 Box select: hold your finger still for about half a second (light vibration), then drag a rectangle — every army of yours inside is selected. 📣 Rally button (left edge): call the 5 or 10 nearest camps, or 🚩 General Mobilization, to a point you tap; ranches and training grounds automatically send troops to garrison the camps you emptied. Tap the minimap (top right) to jump anywhere.

⚔️ Field battles — fight anywhere

Two hostile armies that meet fight on the spot, using the counters of the terrain where they clash (the side already deployed fights from its position, +12% defense). The loser's 15% survivors flee to the nearest friendly post. Towns and citadels are for garrison & defense — the whole map is the battlefield. Win near one of your camps and your army marches back into it automatically; winning armies that fought together merge into one.

March to any point in a 🌲 forest / 🏞️ valley / 🌊 ford and your army sets an ambush right there (garrisons on this terrain use the 🪤 button). Enemies that pass by or attack are caught off guard:

Stack unit and terrain counters for a perfect strike of up to ×10. Each ambush triggers once, then it is exposed. Enemies only ambush in the dark zone outside your territory — inside your lit land you are safe.

⚔️ Battles LAST based on army size (min ~5 s)

When two sides meet, a BATTLE opens: both counters drop over time according to counters, terrain and fortifications. Bigger battles last longer. Tap the battle ring to ZOOM in (🔍 OVERVIEW to zoom out). Nearby armies can join as reinforcements. Your troops in battle get a 🏳️ RETREAT button (12% losses, survivors run back to camp). 🔔 pop-up alerts — tap to jump straight to the fight or ambush.

📋 Order menu

Tap an army → tap a target to choose: ⚔️ Attack · ⛓️ Siege (cuts supply; the town stops producing and starves) · 🕵️ Scout · 🏃 March. Scouts that sweep over an ambush expose it — the enemy also sends 🐎 scouts to hunt your ambushes!

🌾 Supply for every army

Each army carries a 🌾 supply pack: a full pack feeds it for about 150 s of marching (large hosts a little less, camped armies twice as long). Standing near your towns / 🐂 ranches / 🌾 granaries refills it. Winners loot 80% of the loser's supply. Out of supply: troops starve 4% per tick and disband below 20.

🚩 Large armies take time to move. Before leaving a post, 800+ troops must prepare supplies: ~1.5 s for 2 000 troops, 7 s for 10 000, 23 s for 50 000 — longer for a long road, twice as fast from a post with food in stock. They also march slower (2 000 troops −12%, 10 000 −30%, 50 000 −42%). The order menu shows the prep time, the march time and whether the food will last. If the enemy reaches the gates while you are still preparing, the troops go back inside and defend.

🐂 Supply convoys. When an army in the field drops below 45% supply, the nearest post of yours with food (within about 6 000 px) sends a convoy along the roads; bigger armies cost more stock. An enemy army next to a convoy captures it — guard your supply road, and raid theirs. Too far from any depot? Pull back, or capture a 🌾 granary / 🐂 ranch near the front.

📦 Troop cap

The top bar shows troops / supply capacity. Capacity comes from your camps, ranches (+800), granaries (+500) and training grounds (+300). At the cap, camps stop producing troops. Over the cap, the extra troops eat your granaries and armies burn supply twice as fast; once granaries run dry, 1.5% desert every 5 s. Camps also refill more slowly as they fill up. To grow, conquer more land!

🏛️ Civilizations

Before each battle you choose your nation from 44 historical civilizations (or roll 🎲 Random): Đại Việt of the Trần, Tang China, Tokugawa Japan, Goguryeo, the Mongol Empire, Rome, the Maya, Egypt… Each has a signature unit that its camps train and that fights +15–25% harder, plus its own strengths — faster cavalry, tougher forts, deadlier ambushes, forest or mountain troops, bigger supply.

⚔️ Unit types

Every army is one unit type, shown on its banner. Tap an army to see what it ▲ beats and what it is ▼ weak to on the ground it stands on. 🐎 Knights rule open ground but are helpless in forests · 🔱 Spearmen stop cavalry in rough terrain · 🏹 Archers and 🎯 Crossbowmen shred infantry but bounce off 🛡️ Shieldbearers · 🪓 Blademasters break shields · 🗡️ Assassins hunt archers and slip through forest and mountain · ⚔️ Swordsmen are solid all-rounders. ⚒️ Training grounds re-equip an army to their unit type.

🗺️ Terrain & roads

The map is open plains crossed by roads, with forests, mountain ranges, deserts, valleys and one river. 🐎 Cavalry and large armies (2,000+ troops) can only move on open ground and roads — they must go around forests and mountains. Smaller infantry forces can cut through forest and mountain, slowly. The river can only be crossed at a ⛩️ ford. Roads are 12% faster for everyone. So: hold the roads, and hide infantry in the woods beside them to ambush what must pass.

👑 Game modes

⚔️ 1 vs 1: a 10-stage campaign of rising difficulty: from stage 2 the enemy sets AMBUSHES, from stage 6 it only attacks with overwhelming force, and stage 10 is a master strategist. 🌏 WARLORDS: 37 nations on a map 12× larger, auto-saved so you can quit and continue later. Nations judge the balance of power: if you grow too strong they ally against you and send joint armies; if you are weak they fight each other. Propose or break alliances by tapping another nation's camp. 📶 P2P: two players over Wi-Fi by exchanging connection codes (browsers do not support Bluetooth).

👑 Capital: losing captured camps only costs you those posts. If your capital falls while you still own other camps, the capital moves; you lose only when you have no camp left.

🌫️ Fog of war & scouting

Your territory (every post you hold and the roads between them) stays lit; everything else is dark. You never see enemy numbers — even right next to them — only silhouettes. Tap an enemy post (with nothing selected) to send a 🕵️ scouting party (max 2): it takes real time to arrive, reveals exact numbers for 30 seconds, then you only keep old intel like ~536?. Scouts also reveal the area along their route.

🐂 Economy

🐂 Ranches produce troops over time · ⚒️ Training grounds: merge an army into one and the whole force switches to its unit type. Capture enemy farmland to cripple giant armies!
