## Full description (Google Play / App Store)
Command your meme legion in a chibi war for the ages!

Loan The Meme is a tap-to-command, real-time strategy game built for one thumb and big brains:

⚔️ REAL BATTLE TACTICS — spears counter cavalry, archers shred infantry, shields block arrows. Terrain flips every matchup: cavalry rules the plains, infantry owns the forests and mountains.
🌫️ FOG OF WAR — enemy numbers stay hidden until your scouts reveal them. Send riders, expose ambushes, strike where they're weak.
🪤 AMBUSHES ×2–×10 — hide armies in forests, valleys and river fords. One devastating spring per trap.
🌾 SUPPLY LINES — every army eats. Raid granaries, loot the defeated, besiege fortresses and starve them out.
🏰 SIEGES — surround a citadel to cut its supplies, or storm the walls head-on.
🗺️ 40 HANDCRAFTED MAPS — 20 per mode, with roads, rivers crossable only at fords, and randomized headquarters.
🌏 BATTLE ROYALE — 34 AI warlords fight each other in real time. Conquer a capital and its whole nation defects to you. Survive to the end!
📶 P2P DUELS — battle a friend device-to-device over Wi-Fi. No accounts, no servers.
📵 100% OFFLINE — no ads, no tracking, no internet required (except optional P2P).

Easy to learn, ruthless to master. The warring states won't unify themselves!
