# How to Play — Loan The Meme (English Guide)

A real-time chibi war-strategy game. Command armies with taps: scout through the fog of war, lay ambushes, cut supply lines, besiege fortresses, and storm the enemy headquarters.

## Game modes
- **👑 Boss Battle → 1 vs 1:** a 5-stage campaign against one AI warlord.
- **👑 Boss Battle → Battle Royale ("Quần Hùng"):** ~34 AI nations fight each other on one map. A nation whose headquarters falls surrenders entirely to its conqueror — be the last one standing.
- **📶 P2P (2 players):** connect two devices directly over Wi-Fi/Internet by exchanging a connection code (host is blue). Browsers do not allow Bluetooth play.
- **20 maps per mode.** Each map has its own layout — your camp spawns in a different place every map.

## Controls
- **Tap a blue army/town → tap anywhere** on the map. A command menu appears: **⚔️ Attack · ⛓️ Besiege · 🕵️ Scout · 🏃 March**.
- Tap **empty ground** to march there and set up a field camp. Camping in **forest / valley / river banks** automatically sets an **ambush**.
- **Double-tap** a camped army to select the whole nearby group.
- Tap an **enemy army** to chase it; tap a **battle circle** to zoom in; **🏳️ RETREAT** button pulls your troops out (they flee home with 12% losses, immune to re-engagement for 3 s).
- Tap the 🔔 notifications (top-left) to jump straight to the action. 🔍 button zooms back out.

## Combat
- Battles play out **over time** (min ~5 s; the bigger the armies, the longer the fight). Reinforce by sending more armies into the circle.
- **Counters (base):** Spears beat Cavalry ×1.45 · Sabers beat Shields ×1.45 · Archers beat Spears ×1.35 · Cavalry beats Archers ×1.35 · Shields block arrows (×0.6 received).
- **Terrain modifies everything** (applied where the defender stands): Plains favor Cavalry & Shields; Forest/Mountain flip it — infantry beats cavalry & archers there; Valleys +22% and river fords +35% defense. Roads are fast; off-road forest ×0.58 and mountains ×0.42 speed; rivers can only be crossed at **⛩️ fords/bridges**.
- **Fortifications:** Village ×1.25 · Fort ×1.5 · Citadel ×1.8 defense (infantry/archer garrisons +20% more).
- **Ambush ×2–×10:** hidden troops strike enemies passing within range; one strike per ambush, then it is revealed. Enemy scouts can expose your ambushes — and yours can expose nothing less.

## Supply (Food) — armies starve without it
- Every army carries a 🌾 supply bar (100). Bigger armies consume faster.
- Refill near any friendly town, 🐂 ranch, or 🌾 **granary**. Winners **loot 80%** of the loser's remaining supplies.
- At 0 supplies an army withers and **disbands below 20 troops**. Don't march across the continent without supply stops!
- **Siege (⛓️):** surround a fortress to cut its production and starve its garrison — or assault it directly, your choice.

## Victory
- **1 vs 1 / P2P:** capture the enemy headquarters.
- **Battle Royale:** survive until your nation is the last with a standing headquarters.
